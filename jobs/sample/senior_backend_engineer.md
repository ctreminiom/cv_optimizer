# Senior Backend Engineer

**Company:** Acme Cloud (synthetic example)
**Location:** Remote (LATAM)
**Modality:** Remote
**Seniority:** Senior
**Contract:** Full-time

## About the role

We're hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to own our payment-processing services. You'll design and ship distributed systems handling millions of transactions per day, partner closely with product and data teams, and mentor mid-level engineers on the team.

## What you'll do

- Design, build, and operate high-throughput backend services in Go or Python.
- Own end-to-end delivery: API design, data modeling, deployment, monitoring.
- Lead architecture reviews and mentor 2–3 mid-level engineers.
- Partner with product to scope work; write technical RFCs for significant changes.
- Drive on-call rotation and post-incident reviews.

## Must-have requirements

- 5+ years building backend services in Go, Python, or Java (Go preferred).
- Strong distributed-systems fundamentals: queues, consensus, idempotency, retries.
- Production experience with PostgreSQL and at least one message broker (Kafka, NATS, RabbitMQ).
- Comfort with AWS or GCP infrastructure-as-code (Terraform).
- Track record mentoring engineers and driving architecture decisions.

## Nice to have

- Payments domain experience (PCI-DSS, ledgering, fraud signals).
- Experience with gRPC and protobuf.
- Open-source contributions or technical blog posts.

## Salary range

USD 80,000 – 130,000 / year (location-adjusted)

## Tech stack

Go, Python, PostgreSQL, Kafka, AWS, Terraform, Datadog, gRPC
